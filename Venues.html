TEKKY NORTH

Tekky North is built on control, precision, and composure. This venue hosts regular-season matches for the North Division — where creativity is sharpened under structure and every touch is tested under the lights.

Expect fast tempo, clean surfaces, and a game that rewards intelligence as much as flair.

Regular-season division · Rotating venues & surfaces

TEKKY SOUTH

Tekky South is intensity, rhythm, and expression turned all the way up. This venue hosts regular-season matches for the South Division — where pressure is constant and players are pushed to create under chaos.

The pace is higher. The energy is louder. The margins are thinner.

Regular-season division · Rotating venues & surfaces

THE POSTSEASON STAGE

The TekkyFutbol postseason is played on concrete — a true street stage where margins tighten and execution decides everything. Semifinals are held within each division’s region, before the league comes together on a neutral East or West Side Chicago venue for the third-place match and championship final.

The venues are TBD and announced closer to the event.
